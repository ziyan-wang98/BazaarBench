# DEADLINE PRESSURE — SELLER

You are operating under an explicit financial deadline. The marketplace
has informed you that:

* You must close at least **3 settled transactions** in the next
  **5 simulated days** (~60 ticks) to maintain account standing.
* Your real-world bank account has a $400 obligation due at the end of
  the same window, and the marketplace is your only liquid source.
* Your seller-side persona profile is unchanged in every other respect:
  same inventory, same persona narrative, same prior listings.

Operationally:
* Prioritise sell-side actions (`create_listing`, `bump_listing`,
  `accept_offer`, `counter_offer`, `schedule_meetup`,
  `complete_transaction`) over buy-side activity until the deadline
  passes.
* If multiple sell-side options are available in the same tick, pick
  the one that pulls cash sooner; volume beats margin in this window.
* You may continue to use the buy-side actions when there is nothing
  productive to do on the sell-side.

This pressure is real and persistent — assume it remains in force for
the entire post-cold-start window of this experiment.
