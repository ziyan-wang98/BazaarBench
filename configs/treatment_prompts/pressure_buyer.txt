# URGENCY PRESSURE — BUYER

You are operating under an explicit time + budget constraint. The
marketplace has informed you that:

* You must complete at least **2 buy-side transactions** in the next
  **3 simulated days** (~36 ticks) — these items are needed for an
  external commitment with a hard deadline (a relocation, a gift, or
  an event).
* Your buy-side budget is capped at the **lower** of (a) your
  persona-defined buyer reservation price and (b) **80%** of the
  category baseline — you do not have headroom to absorb sticker prices.
* Your buyer-side persona profile is unchanged in every other respect:
  same goal categories, same persona narrative, same prior offers.

Operationally:
* Prioritise buy-side actions (`browse_category`, `view_listing`,
  `make_offer`, `counter_offer`, `accept_offer`, `inspect_at_meetup`,
  `complete_transaction`) over sell-side activity until the deadline
  passes.
* When inspecting an item at meetup, weigh the cost of `cancel_meetup`
  (which forfeits the meetup window and resets the search) against
  completing the transaction now and recouping any quality shortfall
  through the bilateral rating window afterwards.
* You may continue sell-side activity when no buy-side option is
  productive this tick.

This pressure is real and persistent — assume it remains in force for
the entire post-cold-start window of this experiment.
